# Tutorial for Dr. Nayak: the 1:1 meeting record in Hippo

Target length 3 to 4 minutes. Screen recording of the real app with voiceover.
Nothing here asks him to learn a new process. The whole pitch is that it is his
letter, already filled in, that does the retyping for him.

Recorded as the program director, on the University of Manitoba Urology program,
with Dr. Sidhom's July 16 2025 letter already in as the previous meeting.

---

## 1. What this is (0:00 to 0:15)

**On screen:** the signed July 2025 PDF, then dissolve to the same content in Hippo.

**Voiceover:**
> This is your semi-annual resident meeting letter. Same sections, same order, same
> wording. The difference is that last meeting is already on the page, and you never
> retype anything.

---

## 2. Finding the resident (0:15 to 0:35)

**On screen:** the PD dashboard, the cohort list, click into Dr. Sidhom.

**Voiceover:**
> Open your program and pick the resident. Everything about them is on one page:
> their case volume, their EPAs, and now their meeting history.

**Hold on:** the "Program director 1:1" block, showing July 16 2025 marked Published.

---

## 3. Starting the meeting (0:35 to 0:55)

**On screen:** click "Start a meeting". The editor opens.

**Voiceover:**
> One button starts the next one. It carries their training year and today's date
> across. If you get interrupted and come back later, this same button reopens the
> record you started rather than making a second one.

---

## 4. The two columns (0:55 to 1:40)

**On screen:** scroll slowly through the eight sections. Linger on Progress in
training and Career planning so both columns are readable.

**Voiceover:**
> Here is the part that matters. Every section shows July on the left and today on
> the right, exactly the way your letter is laid out. Progress in training. Immediate
> goals. Career planning. Concerns from the resident, then from faculty. T-RES. CBD
> levels. Entrada reminders.
>
> The left side is last meeting, and it is read only. You cannot change history by
> accident. The right side is today, and that is the only thing you are filling in.

---

## 5. Carrying a point forward (1:40 to 2:10)

**On screen:** on Career planning, click "Carry" on Andrology, then on Endourology.
Show them appearing on the right. Then edit one of them slightly.

**Voiceover:**
> When something has not changed, you do not retype it. Press carry and it moves
> across. Then edit it if the wording has moved on. That is the whole trick: the
> things that stay the same cost you one tap each.

---

## 6. Goals, and what actually happened (2:10 to 2:50)

**On screen:** scroll to Immediate goals. Show the three July goals, each with the
four buttons underneath. Click Met on "Attend CUA and AUA", Partly on
"Enhance and expand reading efforts", Carried on the OR planning one.

**Voiceover:**
> Immediate goals works slightly differently, and this is the one thing the app asks
> for that the paper does not. Under each goal you set in July there are four buttons:
> met, partly, not met, carried.
>
> On paper, goals get restated every six months and nobody ever records whether they
> happened. Here you answer that in one tap, and next July you can see the whole run
> of them. It will not let you publish until every goal from last time has an answer.

---

## 7. Exam results and curriculum (2:50 to 3:25)

**On screen:** scroll to the Mini-QUEST table showing all ten sittings. Add a new row
to show how. Then the Core Curriculum list with Practice Management flagged.

**Voiceover:**
> Your exam tables carry forward on their own. Every Mini-QUEST sitting you have ever
> entered is here, and you add the newest one on the bottom row. Away and post call go
> in as text, the same as you write them now.
>
> Same for the core curriculum and the QI modules. A module ticked in PGY-1 stays
> ticked. Anything still outstanding past its year gets flagged, which is the one you
> highlight in yellow by hand today.

---

## 8. Publishing (3:25 to 3:50)

**On screen:** click "Publish this record". Show the record switching to read only.

**Voiceover:**
> When you are done, publish. The record locks, and next time you open a meeting for
> this resident, today becomes the left hand column.
>
> Your signature line stays blank. The app will never sign anything for you.

---

## 9. What is coming (3:50 to 4:05)

**On screen:** back on the resident page, the meetings list now showing two entries.

**Voiceover:**
> Next up is the PDF in your letterhead, sent straight to the resident, and they tap
> once to confirm they have read it. No scanning, no chasing signatures.

---

## Notes for recording

- Record at 1440 wide or more so both columns are legible. Below 900 pixels the
  layout stacks on purpose, which is right on a phone but wrong for this video.
- Use the seeded July 2025 record. It is Dr. Sidhom's real letter, so the content on
  screen is the content Dr. Nayak already recognises.
- Do not show the Mini-QUEST percentile figures in any copy of this that leaves the
  department. They are a real resident's exam results.
- Keep the cursor slow on sections 4 and 6. Those are the two ideas that have to land.

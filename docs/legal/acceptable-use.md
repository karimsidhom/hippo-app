# Acceptable Use Policy

This policy applies to every user of Hippo. By signing in you agree
to it. Programme directors should circulate this policy at programme
onboarding so every resident, fellow, and attending knows the rules
before they log their first case.

## Core principle

Hippo is a professional tool for surgical residents, fellows, and
attendings. The tone, content, and conduct expected on Hippo is the
same as in any surgical department's M&M conference: candid,
educational, respectful, and absolutely confidential about patients.

## What you must not do

### Patient identifiers — never

You **must not** include any of the following in any field on Hippo,
even in a free-text reflection or community pearl:

- Patient name (full, first, last, or initials beyond what's needed
  for medical clarity — even "MRS J.S." is not OK)
- Manitoba PHIN, Ontario / other provincial health card numbers
- MRN or any institutional patient identifier
- Date of birth (full or partial)
- Address, postal code, or any geographic descriptor narrower than
  "Winnipeg" / city
- Photographs of the patient or any imaged identifier
- Any details so specific that a colleague could identify the
  patient — e.g. "the only liver transplant we did last week"

Hippo automatically screens for the most common patterns and will
block your save. **Do not try to bypass it.** Attempts to bypass the
screen are a security incident and will be reported to your
programme director.

### Misuse of patient stories

Even properly de-identified, do not use Hippo to vent about,
ridicule, or otherwise show disrespect toward patients. The same
professional standards that apply at the bedside apply here.

### Misrepresentation

Do not misrepresent your role in a case (e.g. logging yourself as
"Independent" when you weren't). The audit log captures every edit;
deliberate misrepresentation is academic dishonesty.

Do not impersonate another resident, attending, or programme staff.

### Plagiarism in pearls

Pearls in the community feed are *your* clinical observations or a
clearly-attributed quote from literature. Do not republish another
surgeon's content as your own.

### Harassment

The community / leaderboard surfaces are professional spaces. Any
form of harassment, racism, sexism, homophobia, transphobia,
ableism, or threats results in immediate suspension and a referral to
your programme director. Hippo cooperates fully with any subsequent
investigation.

### Sharing your account

Don't share your password. Don't let another resident "log a case
for you" via your account. If you need someone to add a case on your
behalf, ask Hippo support; we have a documented surrogate-entry path
that preserves the audit trail.

### AI misuse

Hippo's dictation, EPA-suggest, and pearl-draft features are
educational aids. They are not a substitute for your clinical
judgment. **You** are responsible for what you save. Do not
copy-paste raw AI output into a hospital chart without reviewing it.

## What we expect of programme directors

If your programme runs Hippo, you commit to:

- **Education before enforcement.** Train residents on the
  acceptable-use policy at programme onboarding.
- **Respond proportionately.** A first-time PHI bypass attempt is a
  teaching moment, not necessarily a misconduct case. A repeated or
  malicious attempt is.
- **Use programme dashboards for the resident's benefit.** The
  cohort-level data on `/pd-dashboard` exists to identify residents
  who need help, not to surveil residents who are doing fine.
- **Don't pry into private community content.** Pearls, friend
  feeds, and 1:1 messages are between the residents who choose to
  see them. Programme directors do not have access to this content
  and Hippo does not provide a workaround.

## Enforcement

For users:
- First minor violation: written warning, content removed.
- Repeated minor violations or any major violation: account
  suspension pending review with the programme director.
- Patient identifier bypass attempts confirmed as deliberate:
  immediate suspension and referral to the programme.

For programmes:
- A programme that uses Hippo to surveil residents in violation of
  the trust principles above will be offboarded. We will refuse to
  serve programmes that compromise resident psychological safety.

## Reporting

To report a violation, email `conduct@hippomedicine.com`. Reports are
confidential. Reports related to safety (patient or resident) are
escalated to a human within 4 h.

## Policy updates

Material updates to this policy will trigger a re-acceptance prompt
at next sign-in. Minor clarifications will be announced via the
in-app notification feed.
